# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Local and international people planning to attend PyCon Cameroon 2027 in Douala.

## Product Purpose

Help prospective and registered attendees plan conference participation and travel with clear, current information.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The Attend page covers tickets, financial aid, visas and invitation letters, health, money and phones, and common questions.
- The user confirmed that 2027 ticket sales and financial-aid applications are not open yet, and venue details are still being confirmed.
- Dates and venue details that are not confirmed must remain clearly labeled as undecided.
- 2026 ticket prices and aid details describe the prior edition and must remain historical.
- Visa, entry, and health information can change. Keep links to official sources and avoid implying advice replaces current official guidance.

## Evidence on Hand

- Attendee guidance and historical 2026 prices: `app/attend/page.tsx`.
- 2027 dates, venue, and ticket/aid status source: `lib/site.ts`.
- Official Cameroon eVisa portal: `https://www.evisacam.cm`.
